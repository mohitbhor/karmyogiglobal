Our Digital Platforms

Technology Bridging Research & Practice

We've developed two complementary platforms that bring ICAR research to farmers' fields and enable evidence-based agricultural advancement

DARPAN™

Research Platform

DARPAN™ (Digital Agriculture Research Platform for Analysis & Normalization) is a secure, India-hosted platform for agriculture research institutions to standardize and analyze crop trial data.

  • Secure capture of crop trial data across locations
  • Multi-season data standardization and normalization
  • Consistent evaluation frameworks for research
  • Comparative analysis across trials and geographies
  • Scalable insights for evidence-based recommendations
  • India-hosted with complete data sovereignty
  • Purpose-built for ICAR and research institutions

UNNATI

Farmer Platform

A digital agronomy platform that empowers farmers with SOP-based crop management, timely interventions, and FPO-level reporting through close agronomist & pathologist collaboration

  • SOP-based crop management guidance
  • Timely intervention alerts and recommendations
  • Cluster and FPO-level performance reporting
  • Close collaboration with field agronomists & pathologists
  • Improved productivity and consistency tracking
  • Market readiness compliance monitoring
  • 100% Free and accessible to all farmers

Our Approach

Respecting Tradition, Enabling Progress

We believe in augmenting farmers' traditional knowledge with modern research, not replacing it. Our mission is to bridge the gap between agricultural science and field practice.

Our Approach for Farmers

Guided Cultivation with Assured Quality

Farmers receive crop-wise, stage-wise guidance based on research-backed SOPs, ensuring better yields, consistent quality, and compliance with market requirements.

Stable Income through Planned Production

Season-long crop planning and a 52-week harvest calendar reduce price volatility and enable predictable income instead of distress selling.

Digital Transparency & Handholding

From crop planning to harvest, every activity is digitally tracked, giving farmers clarity, confidence, and continuous agronomist & plant pathologist support.

Our Approach for Buyers

Demand-Led Supply Planning

Buyer demand drives crop selection, variety choice, and acreage planning—ensuring the right produce, in the right quantity, at the right time.

Predictable Quality with Full Traceability

Research-backed SOPs and end-to-end digital traceability ensure consistent quality, compliance, and audit-ready sourcing.

Real-Time Visibility & Reliable Procurement

Live crop-stage visibility, yield forecasting, and weekly harvest projections enable proactive planning and seamless procurement.

100% Digital, 100% Farmer-Centric

Every aspect of our operation—from crop planning to market linkage—is digitized for transparency, efficiency, and farmer empowerment. Technology serves farmers, not the other way around.

UNNATI aligns farmers and buyers through planned production, verified quality, and transparent execution.
